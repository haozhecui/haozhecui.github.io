About

I am a history student based in Japan, with interests in animation history, media circulation, home-media editions, archival materials, and digital preservation.

My current work pays particular attention to Disney's Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs (1937), including its production, regional circulation, promotional materials, version history, and reception in East Asia.

Research Interests

  • Animation and film history
  • Release and circulation history
  • Posters and promotional materials
  • Home-media editions and restoration history
  • Cel-animation production and frame analysis
  • Digital preservation and media metadata
  • Reception of American animation in China and Japan

Current Projects

Snow White Version and Material Notes
A developing research project documenting release materials, home-media editions, frame-level observations, and regional circulation connected with Snow White and the Seven Dwarfs.

East Asian Promotional Materials
A developing catalogue of Chinese- and Japanese-language posters, advertisements, titles, and distribution materials.
